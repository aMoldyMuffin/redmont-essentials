Who We Are

Built for new players.

Redmont Essentials started with a simple idea: make Democracy Craft less overwhelming for newcomers. Starting with $1,200 and a empty inventory is tough — we package the essentials so you can focus on exploring the city, finding a job, and building your life in Redmont.

Whether you need your first pickaxe or want to offload a haul of crops, we're here to help. Our Discord is the fastest way to get support, place custom orders, or check what's in stock.
